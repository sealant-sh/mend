In a session's Change tab, each comment under `Comments <n> not sent` now has `Mark addressed` and
`Dismiss`, so you can take a comment back before it reaches the agent. A dismissed or addressed
comment leaves the list and the next send. A change-level one shows as a card below with `Reopen`; a
line comment's `Reopen` is on its card in the diff.
