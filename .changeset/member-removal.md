Owners can remove members, change roles, take over a project whose creator left, and read the
organization's audit log. Removing a member deactivates the account, revokes its browser sessions,
paired devices and phones, closes its open terminals, tunnels, key bridge and event streams on every
server process, and stops its sessions through the normal flush and checkpoint. The static token now
acts as the operator instead of the oldest account. Invitations, role and visibility changes,
takeovers, folders and references are recorded in the audit log.
