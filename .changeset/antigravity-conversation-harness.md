Antigravity runs as a conversation as well as in a terminal. The web composer and the phone start it
over `agy acp`, and T3 Code lists it as a provider through the gateway. Each turn takes its own
model, thinking level, Plan and permission. A bypass turn's approvals are answered for you; an ask
turn shows them. A message sent during a turn waits for the next one, since Antigravity takes none
mid-turn.
