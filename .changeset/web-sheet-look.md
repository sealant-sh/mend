The web app has a new look, Sheet. It uses cool greys in place of warm paper, Instrument Sans for
text and Geist Mono for ids, paths and commands. The sidebar and session list sit on a grey desk
beside a white page. Dark mode is near-black with a slightly lighter page. Cobalt stays the one
accent. Amber marks what needs you, and red marks failures. Shadows are neutral and lighter.
