Enforce project visibility on every route. A project, session, worktree, change, process or Service
the caller cannot see answers exactly like a missing one, and the check runs before any effect.
Members see shared projects and their own private ones; project settings are for owners and the
project's creator; removal is for owners, or the creator of a private project; only owners change
visibility, and organization owners may stop any session they can see. Machine settings and the
retired queue are the operator's, and adding host mounts needs the operator role. Live events are
filtered to what each account can see, and one closing stream no longer silences the others.
