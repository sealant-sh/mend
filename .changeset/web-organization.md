Settings shows your organization: members with roles, owners' invitation links, folders with their
files and uploads, projects a departed member left behind, and the audit log. Owners change roles,
remove members (the page says what removal does first), take over a departed member's project, and
page back through the audit log. Invitation links open a join page that creates the account; a
signed-in account is told which organization it already belongs to. A removed account's browser is
signed out and told why.
