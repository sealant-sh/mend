opencode has models to pick. The server's catalog lists the Codex models for opencode as it names
them (`openai/gpt-6.1-sol` and the rest, through your ChatGPT login), with the same default, so the
web composer, the desktop app, the phone and `mend models` offer them, and a session records the one
it started on. An operator's own opencode rows are kept, and so is their default.
