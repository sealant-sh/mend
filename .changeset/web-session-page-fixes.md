On a session's page in the web app, sending a message no longer pushes the conversation up: while
you are at the bottom it stays there as your message and the agent's reply arrive, and if you
scrolled up to read it stays where you are and offers the way back to the latest. The Terminal tab
of a conversation opens a shell by itself for the person who types there. The side panel shows the
screen live instead of its size, and the tab line no longer shows a stray scrollbar.
