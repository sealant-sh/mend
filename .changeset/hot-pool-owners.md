Warm hot sessions for each person who recently ran a session in the project and can still see it,
instead of as the first account, and drain a person's standbys once they lose access. Nothing runs
as a stand-in account any more: a session with no owner cannot launch or be steered, the dependency
install runs as whoever changed the install command (or the project's creator), and the retired
queue's runs act as the operator.
