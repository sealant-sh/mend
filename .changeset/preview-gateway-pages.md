The pages the preview gateway answers itself (a preview a person stopped, one that did not start, a
refused or restarted preview, no such preview, not signed in) are now small pages in Mend's look,
light or dark with the browser, with no external assets or script. They keep their words and status
codes. A stopped, refused or did-not-start page links to the preview's project in Mend, where Start
is.
